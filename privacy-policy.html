Privacy Policy — Slop Merge

Last updated: 21 November 2026

App: Slop Merge, by Kineograph

Website: https://kineograph.us

What this app collects

Nothing. Slop Merge collects no personal data. It does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or give your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

What stays on your device

Your game progress — your best score and your saved board — is stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to Kineograph, Google, or anyone else, and it is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Ads, analytics and tracking

Slop Merge has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party software that collects data about you. The only network use is when you choose to tap the kineograph.us link in the game, which opens the Kineograph website in your browser — that website's own privacy practices then apply.

Sharing your score

If you tap Share Result, the game opens your phone's own share sheet so you can send your score to an app you choose (for example WhatsApp or Messages). Kineograph does not see, store, or receive anything you share.

Children

Slop Merge is a puzzle game suitable for general audiences. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, there is no data from children to collect, use, or share.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here, at https://apps.kineograph.us/privacy-policy.html, and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Slop Merge: visit https://kineograph.us.
