Privacy Policy — Slop Merge

Last updated: 28 November 2026

App: Slop Merge, by Kineograph

Website: https://apps.kineograph.us

What this app collects

Slop Merge is designed to collect as little personal information as possible. The app does not require an account or sign-in, and it does not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or any other personal information automatically.

The only personal information the app ever collects is what you choose to send with the Report an issue form: the name and email address you type in, the message you write, and your game details at the time (your score, best score, and rank) so we can understand the problem. We use that only to read and reply to your report. We never sell it, and we never share it with anyone else.

How your report is handled

Your report is sent to Kineograph over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and stored in Kineograph's database. Only Kineograph reads it. If you send a report while you are offline, the game keeps it on your device and sends it the next time you open the game with a connection. Reports are kept only as long as needed to deal with them. You can ask us to delete your report at any time by emailing awards@kineograph.us — tell us the name and email address you used and we will delete it.

Accounts and deletion

Slop Merge has no accounts. There is nothing to sign into, so there is no account to delete. Your game progress lives only on your device (see below).

What stays on your device

Your game progress — your best score and your saved board — is stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to Kineograph, Google, or anyone else, and it is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Ads, analytics and tracking

Slop Merge has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party software that collects data about you. The only network use is when you choose to send a Report an issue form, or tap the kineograph.us link in the game, which opens the Kineograph website in your browser — that website's own privacy practices then apply.

Sharing your score

If you tap Share Result, the game opens your phone's own share sheet so you can send your score to an app you choose (for example WhatsApp or Messages). Kineograph does not see, store, or receive anything you share.

Children

Slop Merge is a puzzle game suitable for general audiences. The only personal information the app collects is from the optional Report an issue form. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before sending a report with your name or email address.

Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be found at: https://apps.kineograph.us/privacy/slop-merge.html.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Slop Merge, or to ask us to delete a report you sent: email awards@kineograph.us.
